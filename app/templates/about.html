About

This is an independent, non-partisan reconstruction of public works in {{ area }}, {{ city }}, built only from public government records. It aims to make the life of each project (planned, contracted, paid, documented, completed) visible and checkable by anyone.

It is not an audit and makes no finding of wrongdoing. Signals and cases are leads for follow-up; many earlier signals were resolved when better documents were read, and that is shown on the cases page.

Using this data

  • Every fact links to its source. Please verify against the source before quoting.
  • Quote confidence labels with the fact: an inferred value is not a confirmed one.
  • Projects can be downloaded as JSON from /api/projects; cases from /api/cases.
