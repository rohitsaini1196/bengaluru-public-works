Bills & documents

BBMP IFMS works-bill service

accounts.bbmp.gov.in/vssWB: every work bill, with its job number, payee, amounts, bill type, deductions, approval steps and attachments (work orders, agreements, Schedule B, bill forms, work slips, measurement books, completion certificates, extension-of-time orders, site photos). The city-wide payment grid lists every RTGS-paid bill since mid-2015.

Tenders

Karnataka Public Procurement Portal

kppp.karnataka.gov.in: tender notices, award values, every bidder from the commercial comparative statements, and tender documents (which give the stipulated completion period).

Older records

OpenCity mirrors

Published exports of BBMP bill registers (2010 onwards) and the 2013–18 BBMP tender lists, on data.opencity.in.

Plans

Archived BBMP documents

Ward works lists, sanction lists and notices preserved on the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine, some read by OCR.
